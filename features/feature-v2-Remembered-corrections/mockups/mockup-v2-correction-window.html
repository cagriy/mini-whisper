Mockup — not shipping code · Feature v2 · “Correction Window” · Accepted 2026-09-05

One window, opened from the menu bar (“Correct Last Dictation…”) or from a History row (“Correct…”). The delivered transcript is selectable; the selection snaps to whole words and becomes Heard. Write and Sounds like are prefilled from it, scope defaults to the dictation’s app, and the preview and impact line update live. Shown: the editing state, the confirmation state after Save, the two degraded variants (no bundle ID, history off), and both entry points. Design language: the app’s light macOS window on the dark review page, 13 pt system type, grouped controls and blue #0a60ff accent as in the accepted v1 Settings and History mockups.
