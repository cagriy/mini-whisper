Start a meeting

Mini Whisper transcribes the meeting live with speaker labels, keeps your typed notes beside it, and writes a summary when you stop.
macOS will ask to allow System Audio Recording when the meeting starts. Mini Whisper captures only the sound other apps play; it never records your screen. If you decline, the meeting continues with the microphone alone.

Meetings

Drawn in the previous, accepted set. It is here only so Cancel and the sidebar have somewhere to go.

What this proposes

  1. Start. Start Meeting, the meeting hotkey and the menu's Start Meeting… all open the chooser as a page of the main window. Cancel returns to the page you came from. Test Call… is a second step of the same page, with Back.
  2. Live meeting. A one-line header: title, mode, capture, elapsed time, cost so far, Pause, Stop. Transcript on the left, Notes with its composer on the right (280 points). A test call swaps Notes for the Script pane (310 points). You can leave for any other page; the recording pill in the sidebar brings you back.
  3. Stop. The page becomes the finished meeting from the previous set, opening on Summary with "Writing the summary…" until it arrives. Copy and Regenerate wake up once there is a summary.
  4. Finished test call. Same page and switch, but it opens on Transcript, where the Score pane sits beside the transcript as it does today, so selecting a scored line still highlights its words.
  5. Paused. Pause turns into Resume, the capture chip reads Paused, and the sidebar pill stops pulsing and shows Paused.
