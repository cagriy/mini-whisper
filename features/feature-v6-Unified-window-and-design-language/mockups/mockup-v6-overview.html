Main window overview

The whole main window at rest: sidebar, Meetings, a finished meeting, Dictations, search results and Settings, in one design language. It is derived from your three reference screenshots (warm surfaces, serif display titles, pill controls, a centred reading column, grouped settings cards) and made Mini Whisper's own with the sky blue and the cloud-and-pulse mark of the app icon. Starting, recording and the small windows come in the next set.

Revision 2. Usage at the foot of the sidebar is a small table: tokens, audio time and cost in their own aligned columns, for Today and Month.

Design language: baseline DESIGN.md 6200af0 plus the references. Intended fonts: New York for display titles, SF Pro for everything else, SF Mono for commands — system fonts, nothing bundled.

What this proposes

  1. Sidebar. Search at the top, then Start Meeting. While a meeting runs, Start Meeting is replaced in place by the recording pill (pulse, title, elapsed time), which returns to the live meeting. Then Meetings, Dictations, Settings. Settings opens its nine sections beneath it and closes again when you leave. Usage sits at the foot as a small table: Today and Month, with tokens, audio time and cost in aligned columns, covering dictations, meetings and summaries.
  2. Meetings row. Title; then the summary's topic headings on one line; then duration, mode, speakers and cost. Start time on the right. A meeting without a summary shows its first words in italics; an old four-section summary keeps its first sentence. Open, Export and Delete appear on hover without moving anything; Rename stays in the right-click menu and on the meeting's title.
  3. Finished meeting. The summary is the page, in a 640-point reading column. Transcript and Notes are one switch away. Copy summary, Regenerate and Export sit top right; Regenerate asks first and shows the cost.
  4. Search. Typing shows one results page grouped Meetings and Dictations; clearing the field returns to where you were.
  5. Settings. A serif title per section, then cards of rows: name and consequence on the left, control on the right. Theme lives in General. The "Open Meetings…" and "Open History…" buttons go, since the sidebar is always there.
