Detail

Summary

Mini Whisper gains two surfaces that show its user their own history: a Timeline page, an all-time scrolling infographic of meetings, dictation and spend, and a foldable stats block in the sidebar above the usage table. Both read from a new numbers-only record, because today only meeting figures reach back in time: dictation history lasts 7 days and cost resets at the month end. The basis is docs/statistics-findings.md, whose five open decisions are closed here. Every day before the record ships is lost for dictation and cost, which is the reason to build it now.

In scope

  • The record. Per-day and per-meeting rows kept for life, per-dictation rows kept for 13 months, numbers only, backfilled once from stored meetings, the current month's usage and the dictation retention window.
  • Counting rules. A deleted meeting's numbers stay in lifetime totals. Recordings under a minute are neither counted as meetings nor shown. Test calls are left out. Clearing dictation history leaves the dictation numbers.
  • Timeline page. All-time, in themed panels: lifetime strip, year calendar with a year stepper, weekly rhythm, talk share, spend, people, dictation payoff, records and milestones. Then a word cloud of most-used words and topics, a dated strand of milestones, and a folded quality section at the foot.
  • Page behaviour. A panel appears only once it has data. A record opens its meeting while that meeting exists. Figures count up and charts draw in when the page opens, with Reduce Motion honoured.
  • Quality section. Test-call accuracy, transcript lost to dropouts and reconnects, plus the new timings, each shown once it has data.
  • Capture ahead. Timing of the wait from key release to pasted text, live transcript delay and summary wait, and a count of dictation attempts that were not delivered.
  • Sidebar block. Above the usage table: the last 30 days and one lifetime line, with the trend as a small bar graph. Unfolded on first launch, then remembered. Folded, only the usage table remains. Hidden while Settings is open. Clicking it opens Timeline.
  • Navigation. A Timeline row between Dictations and Settings, with ⌘3.
  • Settings. A typing speed for time saved, 40 words a minute by default. A Reset statistics control; after a reset the record rebuilds from what is still stored.

Out of scope / deferred

  • Year in review panel, until a calendar year of rows exists.
  • A page-wide range switch (all time, this year, a past year).
  • Opening a day's meetings from the year calendar.
  • Copy as Image and any other sharing of the infographic.
  • Announcing a milestone or record on the meeting's own page.
  • A statistics command and MCP tool for the user's AI assistant.
  • Month-on-month deltas on the Timeline page.
  • Showing recordings under a minute as false starts.
  • The word cloud and quality figures in the sidebar.
  • App-use counts, goals or targets, and checking estimates against provider invoices.

Technical direction

  • No new dependency. Sparkle stays the only third-party runtime dependency and MiniWhisperCore stays dependency-free. Charts come from Apple frameworks or are drawn by hand.
  • Platform. macOS 14, arm64, Swift 6 with complete strict concurrency. Non-UI logic lives in the core package under Swift Testing; every duration is tested on a virtual clock.
  • Storage. The record lives under ~/.config/mini-whisper/, mode 0600, and never leaves the Mac. No analytics.
  • Numbers only. No text, title or name enters the record. People and the word cloud are computed from kept meetings and never copied.
  • Costs. Stored costs are summed, never recomputed from current prices. Every cost shown is labelled an estimate.
  • Local time. Weekday-by-hour figures need the local time the meeting happened in.
  • App state. Fold state and typing speed belong in config.json.
  • Design language. Every colour, size and type style comes from DS. No shadows inside a window. New components are recorded in DESIGN.md.
  • Motion. The page's count-up and draw-in is a deliberate, recorded exception to DESIGN.md's motion rule, and honours Reduce Motion.
  • Sidebar. 248 px wide with the usage table pinned to the foot. The block may grow upward but never crowds the nav rows.
  • Public repository. No figure measured on a real Mac appears in docs, mockups or fixtures.

Design: feature-design-v11-Timeline-and-sidebar-statistics.md

Summary

Mini Whisper gains a numbers-only record of meetings, dictation and estimated spend, and two surfaces that show it: a Timeline page and a foldable block in the sidebar. Today dictation history is pruned after 7 days, daily usage at the month end, and deleting a meeting lowers every total. The record ships first, in a beta, so history accrues before the page exists.

Goals and non-goals

  • Per-day and per-meeting numbers for life, per-dictation numbers for 13 months, surviving deletion, pruning and the month end.
  • A Timeline page from a sidebar row, ⌘3 and the menu-bar menu.
  • A sidebar block for the last 30 days with one lifetime line.
  • Settings › Statistics: typing speed and Reset statistics.
  • Not in this design: year in review, a range switch, sharing, a statistics command for agents, a per-request timing file, any change to the usage table.

Design

Components

MWStats depends on MWMeetings, MWUsage, MWHistory, MWConfig and MWSupport. MWPipeline and MWMeetingPipeline record through two small protocols.

ComponentResponsibility
StatsStoreActor owning the record's four files: seed, record, fold, prune, compact, reset, snapshot
StatsSeedSeeds from stored dictations, the current month's usage and kept meetings
MeetingFiguresA meeting's row from its folder
MeetingReconcilerActor that brings meeting rows and word counts up to date
RecordedUsageWraps the usage store so every cost also reaches the day row
WordTally, WordCache, WordCloudPer-meeting noun counts, their file, and the cloud
TimelineStats, SidebarStats, PeopleStatsPure aggregation for the page, the block and the People panel

Data

FileOne line holdsKept
stats/dictations.jsonlAn attempt: time, app, engine, seconds, words, outcome, wait, cost13 months
stats/days.jsonlAdditive day figures: dictations, words, seconds, undelivered, by app, by hour, spend by purposeFor life
stats/meetings.jsonlA meeting's numbers: duration, words, per-speaker seconds, questions, gaps, cost split, delayFor life, past deletion
word-counts.jsonWord counts per kept meeting, for the word cloudUntil the meeting is deleted

meeting.json gains the start's UTC offset and a timing block. config.json gains the fold state and the typing speed.

Surfaces

  • Timeline: lifetime totals, the year, weekly rhythm, talk share, spend, people, dictation, records with next milestones, words, milestones reached, Quality folded.
  • Each panel waits for a minimum: 10 meetings for the rhythm, 3 for talk share, 20 dictations for the dictation panel, 5 transcripts for the words.
  • A record opens its meeting. One whose meeting was deleted shows its date and "Deleted meeting".
  • Sidebar block: hidden while Settings is open and until data exists; drops its trend, then hides, when the sidebar is too short.

Failure and security

  • A stats failure is logged and never fails a dictation, a meeting or a summary.
  • A torn line after a crash is skipped and dropped at the next compaction.
  • Directory 0700, files 0600. Nothing opens a network connection.
  • A test asserts no transcript text, title or name reaches the record.

Testing

A new MWStatsTests target covers each component alone on fixtures, a virtual clock and an injected calendar. Existing pipeline suites gain one case per dictation terminus and the purpose of each usage add.

Risks

  • The page opens thin for weeks: no dictation or timing panels until data accrues.
  • A missed or doubled write would corrupt totals that cannot be rebuilt; the compile-time purpose and a test per terminus guard it.
  • Talk share and People are only as good as speaker attribution.
  • The word-count file holds words from private meetings, beside their transcripts.
  • 0.9.0, and with it speaker naming, waits for all of v11.

Rollout

  1. Phase A, the record: capture, seed, reconcile, Reset in Settings. Cut as 0.9.0b4.
  2. Phase B, the block and the page: navigation, eight panels, motion, typing speed.
  3. Phase C: People, Words, Quality.
  4. 0.9.0 once phase C has been used on the beta channel.

Detail

Overview

v11 adds a numbers-only record under ~/.config/mini-whisper/stats/ and two surfaces that read it: a Timeline page and a foldable sidebar block. The plan follows the design's three phases, and each ends on a batch boundary where the user can cut a 0.9.0 beta. Every stage is test-first; app-only layout is labelled platform-only/UI, and wiring stages are hybrid with a named check.

Stages

Phase A — the record

  1. MWStats module — registration, StatsTime, JSONLFile, WordCount, recording protocols, FakeStatsRecorder, Log.stats. Package.swift; Sources/MWStats; Tests/MWStatsTests with Fixtures.
  2. Every usage states its purpose — UsagePurpose, required on ProviderUsage; testCall for test calls. MWUsage, ProcessingJob, MeetingBilling, MeetingController and their tests.
  3. Rows, store, RecordedUsage — the four row types, StatsStore (record, spend, upsert, prune, compact, changes), the usage decorator.
  4. Meeting timing fields — Meeting.utcOffsetSeconds, Meeting.timing, DelayStats; MeetingStore.meeting(_:) and modified(_:).
  5. Seed, meta and reset — StatsSeed and the store's needsSeed, seed and reset.
  6. MeetingFigures — a meeting's row from its folder, with no text.
  7. MeetingReconciler — MeetingSource, single-flight reconcile with progress, meetingStopped.
  8. Dictation capture in the job, and the record at launch — one attempt per job terminus, wait from release; AppDelegate seeds or opens and wraps usage; project.yml.
  9. Meeting capture and the reconciler at launch — offset, transcript delay, summary wait, meetingStopped; reconcile at launch.
  10. Dictation capture in the controller — the six termini that never reach the job.
  11. Settings › Statistics with Reset — section, dialog, History row, reset wiring, CLAUDE.md and README privacy.

Phase B — the block and the page

  1. Config keys and Typing speed — timeline_sidebar_folded, typing_wpm, the Settings row.
  2. StatFormat and SidebarStats — number, duration and money text; the block's figures.
  3. Milestones and Streaks.
  4. Timeline report I — totals, the year, weekly rhythm, talk share.
  5. DS tokens, charts and playback — Radius.cell, cloudSmall, heat, the chart components, PanelPlayback.
  6. Timeline report II — spend, dictation, records, milestones.
  7. TimelineModel and TimelineSource — states, replay and return, opening a record.
  8. Timeline page and panels 1–5, 7, 8, 10 — platform-only/UI.
  9. Navigation — Destination.timeline, sidebar row with ⌘3, menu row, back pill.
  10. Sidebar block and fold handle — ViewThatFits, freshness, CLAUDE.md layout, README window.

Phase C — People, Words, Quality

  1. PeopleStats — adds MWVoices and MWNaming to MWStats.
  2. WordTally and WordCloud — NaturalLanguage nouns with a fallback.
  3. WordCache and the reconciler's word step.
  4. Timeline report III — People, Words, Quality.
  5. Word counts in the app — delete hook, reset clears words, the full Settings line.
  6. Panels 6, 9 and 11 — WordCloudView, CLAUDE.md, README privacy.
