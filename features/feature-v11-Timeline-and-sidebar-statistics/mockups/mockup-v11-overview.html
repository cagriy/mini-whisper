Timeline overview

The new Timeline page in the main window: an all-time infographic of meetings, dictation and estimated spend, in the order the page would scroll. Each panel is shown only once it has data, so the page also has a just-updated state and an empty state. The sidebar here carries only the new Timeline row; its stats block and the Settings controls come as the next two sets.

Design language: DESIGN.md 6c9891e. Charts are plain shapes drawn from its tokens. Intended fonts: New York for the title and the figures, SF Pro for everything else — system fonts, nothing bundled.

What this proposes

  1. Order. Lifetime totals, the year, weekly rhythm, talk share, spend, people, dictation, records with the next milestones, words, milestones reached, then Quality folded at the foot.
  2. Open panels, not cards. Each panel sits on the canvas under a hairline with an SF heading. Totals use the serif at the size of a test call's score; figures inside panels use the serif at title size.
  3. Where the extra figures go. Back-to-back meetings with the rhythm; your speaking pace and questions you asked with talk share; cost per recorded hour with spend.
  4. One switch for two measures. The year and the rhythm show meetings, and switch to dictation, instead of encoding both in one cell.
  5. Motion. A panel plays once when it first comes into view: figures count up over 0.9 s, bars grow over 0.6 s, cells and words fade in over 0.45 s, each staggered by 12 to 45 ms. Panels already in view at opening start 80 ms apart. With Reduce Motion everything is final at once. Use Replay opening to see it again.
  6. Thin start. "Just updated" is the page on the day the record begins: meetings from what is stored, one unsplit month of spend, no dictation panel until 20 dictations, and a status line while stored meetings are read.
  7. Records. A record whose meeting is kept opens it. One whose meeting was deleted shows its date and "Deleted meeting" and is not a button.
