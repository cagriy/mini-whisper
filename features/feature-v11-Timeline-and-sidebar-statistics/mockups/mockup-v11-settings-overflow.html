v11 · Settings sidebar at the minimum height

Accepted 2026-11-25: usage-hidden. scrolling-sections and taller-minimum not chosen.

With Settings open the sidebar lists eleven sections. The Statistics section (0.9.0b4) and the Timeline row (Phase B) take it from 598 pt to 657 pt, so at the 900 × 600 minimum window the search pill rises under the traffic lights and the usage table's Month row is cut off. At the 1080 × 720 default it fits. Three ways to fix it, drawn at the minimum size. Design language: DESIGN.md (23a9b24); sizes from the code: rows 30 pt, sections 24 pt, gaps 10 pt.

Today, at 600 pt

The column needs 657 pt; 57 pt do not fit. The stats block is already hidden while Settings shows, so it plays no part.

scrolling-sections · recommended

The sections scroll

Only when space runs short, the destinations and sections scroll between Start Meeting and the usage table, which stays pinned. Nothing changes at the default size. Trade-off: at the minimum height the last sections sit below a fade until you scroll.

taller-minimum

The window's minimum grows

The minimum window height rises from 600 to 660 pt, so the whole column always fits and nothing in the sidebar changes. Trade-off: every page loses 60 pt of the smallest window, including on small displays.
