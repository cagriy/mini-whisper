What changed

  • Third row. Words transcribed and words dictated join meetings, hours recorded, dictations and time saved against typing.
  • Fold. The handle from Headline and bars replaces the header row. Nothing of the block stays when folded.
  • Type. As in Figures grid: figures in SF semibold 14, labels at the usage table's size.
  • Fit. The block is 218 px tall. At the 600 px minimum window height it leaves 20 px above it, 14 px while a meeting records. If it ever cannot fit, it drops the trend first, then hides.
  • Hidden while Settings is open and until any data exists, as the switches above show.
