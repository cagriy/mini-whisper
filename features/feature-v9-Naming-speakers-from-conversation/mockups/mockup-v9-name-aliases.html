Where the misheard spellings of your name are kept

That's me on a suggestion records its name as a mishearing of yours ("Neve" for Niamh), so it is never suggested for anyone again. These options show where you see, remove and add those spellings: as removable chips under Your name, as a list in the Speaker names card chosen in set 2, or as one line under Your name that opens a popover.

Follows DESIGN.md: settings cards with hairline rows, quiet small pills, hover-revealed actions as the transcript uses, and the dialog card for popovers. Option A adds a removable variant to the Chip component; B and C reuse existing parts. Names are placeholders. Remove and add work here; press Return to add.

The same in all three options

  • How a spelling gets there: That's me on a suggestion, in a live or stopped meeting, or typed here. Typing one lets you head off a mishearing you already know about.
  • Matching ignores case and accents: "neve" and "Névé" match "Neve". A spelling equal to Your name, a blank, or "Speaker 3" is not added.
  • What a spelling does: in a remote call it is never suggested for another speaker, since your microphone is already labelled with your name. In a room it is shown as your own name. The spellings go to Anthropic with Your name, so Claude knows them too.
  • Remove takes effect at once and only for later suggestions; names already given in past meetings stay as they are.
  • Kept with your settings, across meetings, and kept when you change Your name.
