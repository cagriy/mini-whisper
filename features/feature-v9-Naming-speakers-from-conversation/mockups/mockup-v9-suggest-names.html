Where "Suggest names" sits on a stopped meeting

A stopped or stored meeting can ask Claude for names for the speakers still called Speaker N, over the whole transcript, at any time. The suggestions land in the transcript with the hover card chosen in set 1. The three options differ on where the action sits: a line at the top of the Transcript tab, a pill in the top bar beside Regenerate, or a link after the header chips. Each shows the same five states.

Follows DESIGN.md: pill buttons in the top bar, Chips, the Summary · Transcript · Notes switch, the status line, speaker colours, and provisional names with their quiet actions. Names, times and costs are placeholders. Use the states above the options, or click the action itself.

State

A · Line on the Transcript tab transcript-line · Accepted 2026-11-22

A status line at the top of the Transcript tab, shown only while some speaker has no name: "3 speakers have no name", with Suggest names in it. The same line then shows progress and the outcome. Trade-off: it appears only where the names land and only when it is useful, but it is out of sight on the Summary tab, where a stopped meeting opens.

B · Pill in the top bar top-bar-pill · Not chosen

Suggest names sits beside Regenerate, as one more thing you can ask Claude to do with this meeting. While it runs the pill reads "Suggesting…", and the outcome shows as a status line on the Transcript tab. Trade-off: visible from every tab and grouped with Regenerate, but the top bar grows to five controls, and the pill is disabled, not hidden, when every speaker has a name.

C · Link after the chips chips-link · Not chosen

A quiet link follows the speakers chip, the place that lists the names it fills in. Progress and outcome replace the link in grey text, and the cost chip updates beside it. Trade-off: next to the speaker names and visible from every tab, but the chips row is read-only today, and a link in it is easy to overlook.

The same in all three options

  • Only open speakers are considered: not you, not one you or your voices settings named, not one with a suggestion standing. "Suggest names" again later never replaces a standing suggestion with the same name.
  • Not offered on a test call, or while a request for this meeting is already running. Works with the Suggest names switch off; needs the Anthropic key.
  • Outcomes: "2 names suggested." · "No new names found." · "Names not suggested: <reason>." The line stays until you leave the meeting or run it again.
  • Billed to this meeting: the cost chip includes it (about a cent for an hour's meeting).
  • Suggestions behave as live ones: the "?" card shows the quote; Accept, Edit, Dismiss and That's me apply.
