Where the two naming switches sit

Settings › Meetings today, plus Suggest names from the conversation (on by default, needs an Anthropic key) and Apply self-introductions without asking (off by default). All three options use the same switches and wording. They differ on one axis: where the switches sit. A is a card of their own, B joins the Summary card, which already needs the Anthropic key, and C puts them in the Engine card next to the voice switches.

Follows DESIGN.md: settings cards with hairline rows, the switch, inset boxes as v8's voice switches use, and footnotes that state what a setting does. Prices are placeholders. The new or changed card has a faint blue outline, which is page chrome, not app styling. The switches work: flip them, or try the no-key state above the options.

Try a state Turning off Suggest names dims the second switch; with no key both are off and dimmed and the key hint shows.

The same in all three options

  • What leaves the Mac, said on the row. The description under Suggest names is the disclosure; there is no separate dialog. It names Anthropic, the transcript and the People names, and says it happens during the meeting.
  • No key: both switches show off and cannot be moved; the hint is the summary's own. Adding a key restores the stored setting, which is on unless you turned it off.
  • Takes effect at once, both ways, including in a meeting already running. Turning it off mid-meeting keeps the suggestions already shown.
  • Apply self-introductions is dimmed while Suggest names is off. Only a speaker saying their own name is applied; every other kind of evidence stays a suggestion.
  • "Suggest names" on a stopped meeting still works with the switch off: it is a click, not a background feature. It still needs the key.
  • No window slider: every request sends the whole meeting so far; prompt caching keeps it to about 3 cents a meeting.
