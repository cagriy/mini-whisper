Review of the current animation

The shipping overlay has 24 white dots, up to 276 proximity links, a 20-point corner radius, and a 70% black background. Audio uses fast attack and slow decay, with up to 130 points of target displacement. Processing rotates the particle homes at 3 radians per second; completion collapses the dots and fades the card. The angular links make activity visible, but their changing density can compete with the transcript. The alternatives retain the square card and monochrome palette while exploring simpler motion.

The constellation above is an illustrative reconstruction from ConstellationSimulation.swift and ConstellationLayer.swift, not a capture or exact physics port. Alternative motion and timing are proposals. Complete is held for inspection; the shipping card dismisses. All audio is simulated. No microphone access or network requests.
