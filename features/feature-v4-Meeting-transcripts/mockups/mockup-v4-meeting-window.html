Mockup — not shipping code · Feature v4 · “Meeting Window” · Accepted 2026-11-09

The one window a meeting lives in, shown in its three states top to bottom: the chooser a hotkey press opens, the live recording view, and the finished meeting with its summary. One window per meeting, 920×620, reopened from the menu bar if closed; recording never stops on close. What to judge: placement of the notes pane, how speakers and times read in the transcript, how connection state and running cost sit in the header, and the summary panel's position above the transcript. Design language: the accepted v1 History and Settings mockups — light macOS window on the dark review page, 13 pt system type, hairline-bordered white controls. Names and text are placeholders.

State 1 · Hotkey pressed, nothing recording yet

The press opens the window on this chooser; nothing is captured until Start. The blue note appears only before the first remote meeting on this Mac, ahead of the macOS permission prompt. On macOS 14.0 and 14.1 the Remote card is greyed with “needs macOS 14.2”.

State 2 · Recording, 42 minutes in

Transcript on the left in speaker paragraphs; consecutive segments of one speaker merge under one heading with the time of its first word. Click a name to rename it everywhere. Partial text is grey with a cursor. In a remote call the microphone and system audio are mixed into one stream; words spoken while the microphone is active are attributed to you on the Mac. The connection dropped and is reconnecting; the gap marker shows where. Notes on the right carry the meeting time of their first keystroke; Enter adds one.

Stop closes the session, bills the minutes into Today/Month and sends the transcript and notes to Claude Sonnet 5.5. The summary sits above the transcript so the result is the first thing seen; the transcript stays editable below: double-click a paragraph to edit its text, right-click a name to move that segment to another speaker. Export buttons open the save panel. When the summary failed, the panel is one line with the reason and a Summarise button (inset).
