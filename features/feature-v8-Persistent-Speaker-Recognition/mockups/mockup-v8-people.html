The People list: who the app remembers, and forgetting them

A new Settings section, People, placed after Meetings in the sidebar. It lists everyone whose voice is remembered, with which engines know them and when they were last heard, and it is the one place to rename, merge or forget a person, or to forget everyone.

Follows DESIGN.md (the sidebar's Settings sections, a page column under a serif title, a card with hairline rows, hover actions that keep their space, quiet pill buttons, the Dialog card over a scrim with one filled button). Placeholder names and dates. Live: switch the state, hover a row, try Rename, Merge into… and Forget.

2 · The three confirmations

Each is the Dialog card over the window, with Cancel and one filled button. They are shown here at rest; press the actions above to see them in place.

Forget, from a row

Forget Alice Example?

Her stored voice, her voice data in past meetings and her lines in the match log are deleted. Transcripts keep the name as text.

CancelForget

Merge into…, after choosing the person to keep

Merge Alice E. into Alice Example?

Alice Example keeps her name and gets both voices. Past meetings are not changed.

CancelMerge

Forget everyone

Forget everyone?

Deletes all 5 people, the voice data kept inside every past meeting and the match log. Transcripts keep their names as text. This cannot be undone.

CancelForget everyone

What this proposes

  • Where. "People" is a tenth Settings section, after Meetings. The Meetings page stays the length it is, and the list has room to grow.
  • Page. Serif title "People", one lead line saying what is kept, then a card of rows sorted by last heard, newest first. The group line above the card counts them.
  • Row. Name at 14 px medium. Under it, in grey: the engines that know the person, joined by a middle dot (Local model · Speechmatics Standard · Speechmatics Enhanced), then "last heard" and the date.
  • Your own entry carries a "You" chip, takes its name from Meetings › Your name, and has no Rename and no Merge into…. Other people can be merged into it. Forgetting it says your voice is learned again in your next remote meeting.
  • Row actions appear on hover or keyboard focus at the right, as quiet small pills in reserved space: Rename, Merge into…, Forget. Forget's label is red on hover.
  • Rename opens a popover with the name selected and the line "Past meetings keep the old name." A name another person already has is refused there: "Bob Sample already exists. Use Merge into… instead."
  • Merge into… opens a menu of the other people; choosing one asks for confirmation. The chosen person keeps the name.
  • Forget everyone is a row in its own card under the list, with an outlined button whose label is red.
  • Nobody yet. The cloud-and-pulse mark, "No one yet", one sentence on how people get here, and a small button to the Meetings settings.
  • While a meeting records an info line sits under the title and every action is disabled: "A meeting is recording. Rename, merge and forget are available when it stops."
