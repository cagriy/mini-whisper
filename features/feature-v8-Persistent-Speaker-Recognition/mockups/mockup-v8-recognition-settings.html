The same in both options

  • Off by default. With both toggles off the slider and Advanced are not shown, and the page reads as it does today plus two switched-off rows.
  • What is stored, said on the row. The description under each toggle is the disclosure; there is no separate dialog. It stays visible while the toggle is on.
  • Provider not in use. The toggle stays operable and adds one grey line: "Used when a Speechmatics engine is selected."
  • Cannot be used. No key: the switch is disabled under the engine rows' own hint, "Add a Speechmatics key under Keys to enable". Model fails to load: disabled with "The voice model could not be loaded on this Mac."
  • One slider, eleven steps, centre by default. It sets five of the six Advanced values together. Its caption names the result. Change an Advanced value by hand and the caption reads Custom with a hollow thumb; moving the slider, or Reset, puts every value back on a step.
  • Advanced is closed by default: sure match 0.70 · suggestion 0.50 · margin 0.10 · minimum speech 15 s · audio buffer 120 s · Speechmatics sensitivity 0.80. A value whose route is switched off is dimmed.
  • Changes apply from the next meeting; switching a toggle off takes effect at once.
