The Credits window, and how About opens it

Bundling the WeSpeaker voice model requires crediting it (CC BY 4.0), and Sparkle's licence asks for its notice to ship with the app. A small Credits window lists both. About gets one quiet button that opens it.

Follows DESIGN.md (small windows with a standard title bar and a fixed size, the settings card with hairline rows, quiet small pill buttons, an action that opens something ends in an ellipsis). The component names and licences are the real ones the app must show. Live: press Credits… and the licence buttons.

What this proposes

  • About. One quiet small pill, "Credits…", under the "Powered by" line. The window grows from 300 × 300 to 300 × 330 to hold it; nothing else in it changes.
  • Credits window. A small window like About: standard title bar titled "Credits", fixed at 460 × 440, closed with the red button. One lead line, then a settings card with one row per component.
  • Row. The component's name at 14 px medium with what it does in grey beside it; a paragraph naming who made it and how it is used; a last line with the licence name and a quiet button.
  • WeSpeaker voice model. Credits WeSpeaker, pyannote and Fluid Inference, says it is a modified Core ML conversion, names CC BY 4.0 and shows the licence address as selectable text. "Open Licence…" opens that page in the browser.
  • Sparkle. Its copyright line and the parts it includes. "Show Licence" unfolds the full licence text in a scrolling monospaced box, read from a file shipped inside the app, so it works offline.
  • Opening Credits again brings the same window forward; it does not close About.
