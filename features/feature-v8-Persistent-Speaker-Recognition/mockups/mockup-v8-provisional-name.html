What this proposes

  • Suggestion. The suggested name with a question mark in the name's place: speaker colour, 13 px semibold, 1.5 px dashed underline. "Speaker N" follows in grey 12 px, then the time.
  • Three small quiet pill buttons, right-aligned, on every paragraph of that speaker: Accept, Edit, Dismiss. No outline and a grey label; on hover or keyboard focus the label turns blue over the usual hover fill. "Not Alice" is drawn the same way.
  • Accept makes it a plain name everywhere and remembers the voice when the toggle is on.
  • Edit opens the rename popover under the name: title "Name Speaker 2 (every segment)", the field holding the suggested name, selected. Return or Rename applies what is typed; the voice is then remembered under that name, and the suggested person is not offered for this voice again in this meeting. Leaving the suggested name unchanged is the same as Accept. Clicking the provisional name itself opens the same popover.
  • Dismiss turns it back into Speaker N; that person is not offered for this voice again in this meeting.
  • Narrow columns. Below about 470 px of transcript width the buttons move to their own line under the name; they never shrink or truncate.
  • Applied name. A sure match looks like any other name. Hover or keyboard focus shows a quiet "Not Alice" after the time, in space that is always reserved.
  • Everywhere else (summary, exports, chips, agents) a suggestion still reads Speaker N until accepted or renamed.
