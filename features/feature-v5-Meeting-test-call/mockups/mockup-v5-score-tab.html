Mockup — not shipping code · Feature v5 · “Score Tab” · Option 2 of 3 · round 1 · Not chosen

The meeting window after Stop on a test call, and when a test meeting is reopened. A Score / Summary and transcript switch sits above the meeting. A test call opens on Score: headline figures, a per-speaker table, earlier runs, then every line as heard. The script pane stays on the right. Trade-off: Room for full tables and a line-by-line view; the summary and transcript are one click away, and the switch exists only for test calls. The other options: Score above the summary (score-panel), Score in the script pane (pane-score). All three carry the same figures: word error rate per speaker and overall, the label each speaker got and its purity, latency, skipped lines, and earlier runs of the scenario. What differs between the options: where the score sits against the summary and transcript, and how much shows without a click. Design language: the accepted v4 Meeting Window and Meetings list, the v5 Script pane and TEST tag. Script, names and figures are placeholders.
