Where a test call starts from in the New Meeting window. Every option offers the same three controls: Scenario (Random, or one of 8 named scripts), Engine (only engines with a key; defaults to the Settings engine) and Overlap (off). A test call is always a remote call. What differs: how prominent the test call is in a chooser most people use for real meetings. Design language: the accepted v4 Meeting Window chooser (mockup-v4-meeting-window.html) — light macOS window on the dark review page, 13 pt system type, white hairline controls, blue selection ring. The two menus at the bottom are shared by all options. Names and figures are placeholders.
