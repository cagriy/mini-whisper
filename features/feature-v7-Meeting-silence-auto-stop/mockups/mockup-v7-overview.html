Silence stop: overview

When a meeting has had no transcribed speech for the set time (1 min by default, never in its first 3 min), a floating prompt asks whether to keep recording and stops the meeting a minute later if nobody answers. Shown here: the prompt over a full-screen call, its Settings row, and how the stopped meeting reads afterwards.

Follows DESIGN.md (warm neutrals, sky-blue accent, New York titles over SF Pro, pill buttons, one filled button per dialog, the Dialog card with its shadow). New York and SF Pro come from the system font stack here. Placeholder names and titles throughout.

1 · The prompt

A borderless floating panel, the Dialog card without its scrim, centred across the active display a fifth of the way down, above every window and full-screen call on every Space. It never takes keyboard focus, so it has no Return or Esc shortcut; a click answers it. The bar runs down with the countdown.

3 · The stopped meeting

A meeting that silence stopped carries one more chip in its header, after the duration, every time it is opened. A meeting you stopped yourself, including through Stop now, has no such chip. Transcript and exports are unchanged.

What this proposes

  • The prompt is the Dialog card (380 px, card radius, dialog shadow) as a borderless floating panel with no scrim: centred horizontally on the active display, its top a fifth of the way down.
  • Eyebrow: the resting recording bars and "Recording · <meeting title>". Title in the dialog serif: "No one has spoken for 1 minute" (the minutes follow the setting). Body: "Recording stops in 0:42 unless you keep it going." A 4 px bar in the recording colour runs down with the seconds.
  • Buttons right-aligned: Stop now (outlined, red label) then Keep recording (the one filled accent button). No close box, no keyboard shortcuts.
  • Settings: "Stop after silence" row in the Recording card after Keep meeting audio, a segmented pill Off · 1 min · 2 min · 5 min, default 1 min; its description follows the chosen value.
  • Stopped meeting: a chip "Stopped after 1 min without speech" with a pause-circle symbol, after the duration chip.
